# Satin Mile design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want the vehicle inspected in useful light instead of hidden under heavy gloss effects. The page should make service scope understandable and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Lantern ink — `#17242B`
- Night glass — `#203943`
- Warm beam — `#F0B94C`
- Rinse mint — `#7BC7B7`
- Aluminum — `#E3E8E5`
- Daylight — `#FAFBF8`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Reuse the precision/finish frame. Replace the finish map with a lantern-beam inspection field that focuses attention on cabin, wheels, glass, and paint.

## Signature

A conic lantern beam reveals four inspection zones without becoming a decorative glow effect.

## Critique

The first concept leaned toward vintage lantern branding. The revision uses contemporary inspection-light geometry so the identity stays connected to real detailing work.
